7 Cures for a Lean Purse | 5 Laws of Gold | Conclusion

The Richest Man in Babylon Reflection

7 Cures for a Lean Purse

1. Start to fatten thy purse.
This is the first and crucial step: save at least one-tenth of your earning,dont spend all of it. Basically pay yourself first. This means treating my savings like a bill. No matter how little I earn, I can start with some small amount. It is just the building of habits that counts more than the sum of money during that time.
2. Control thy expenditures.
Arkad says that necessary expenses will rise to meet our income, if we actually let them. This is so true today. A want basically feels like a need. This means tracking my spending, cutting expenses I do not use, and asking, “Do I really need this?” A simple plan allows my money to stay in the purse.
3. Make thy gold multiply.
Saving is not enough the money must put in work. Money should go on to earn more money! This means learning about safe investments. I don't have to start rich to do this, but I can start small and reinvest what I earn, and let time do the heavy lifting.
4. Protect your fortune from being squandered
Do not go after big returns if you cannot handle to lose your capital. Rely on good counsel from money experts and keep away from investments that you do not unserstand. For me, this means avoiding any investments that are too good to be true, or ones that sound suspicious. It means having cash reserves that would allow me not to sell my assets at the wrong moment.
5. Make your residence an efficient piece of property.
Arkad say that people should own a variety of properties for investing purposes. It will help to save money and guarantee better living conditions. For me, this may not be reasonable in some areas. However, this should be understood for a larger idea: one should treat a property as a long-term investment and not just a monthly payment.
6. Prepare for a prosperous future.
We are growing old and might not be able to work. We might need some help at some point. That is why we have to prepare beforehand. For me, this means having pension savings set aside, as well as insurance and a will.
7. Raise your earning capacity.
This is the remedy that a lot of peoples overlook. If you want to earn a larger salary, you need to increase your worth. Gain new information, improve your skills, and be independent. For me, I already try to practice this, aside from my major in game development, I do and also will continue to learn graphic design, video editing and other skills. The more I am able to do, the more I can earn.

5 Laws of Gold

1
The reward of gold is given to those who save at least a tenth of their income. In simpler terms, a small amount of consistent saving goes a long way.
2
The wise investor finds gold working well for him. Money does not grow by just keeping it; it must be put to work to multiply.
3
Gold is retained by the person who takes the advice of wise counsel. Don't act recklessly. Always seek the advice of knowledgeable people when dealing with finances.
4
Gold runs away from the person who throws his money into the unknown. This is fundamental. Never invest your money in a venture that you do not understand, regardless of how appealing it may sound.
5
Gold escapes from the man who gets involved in his desire for instant riches and in the schemes of tricksters.

How I Can Connect This to My Life

I could try starting by keeping ten percent of my earnings. I can create a basic budget. I can acquire one new financial skill at a time. I can just not take on debts for useless purchases. I can seek expert advice before investing my money. I can take measures to insure and save for my family. I can keep on learning and earning more. However, the most important change is a mental one. I must not be saying, “I will begin saving later when I have more money.” I must act now. I should not believe that “This investment will make me rich overnight” anymore.

My Biggest Takeaway

In conclusion, my main takeaway is that wisdom is more important than gold. It is true that gold comes and goes but once I know how to handle money I can earn it and grow it. The book has made it clear to me that wealth is not accumulated with a big move. It is done through a series of boring probably even draining steps, such as saving and budgeting. In short, the main point is that one does not need luck to be successful with finances, but rather need to be disciplined and patient.
