My Three Strongest Traits and Their Academic Impact

1. Persistence
This characteristic motivates me to complete challenging tasks and continue working when something is not clear to me. It increases my academic regularity since I do not give up my work just because it is difficult.
2. Graphic Design
It instructs me on visual hierarchy, organization, and awareness of the audience, which enables me to plan my essays and presentations more clearly. Making a messy design clean gives rise to my motivation of achieving order in my academic endeavors.
3. Video Editing
It develops the ability to be patient and construct narratives. Hence, my writing improves considerably. Furthermore, when I edit, it helps me to express my creativity thus relieving me of the academic burden.

LLMs as a tool not a crutch Essay

Beyond the Crutch: Using Large Language Models as Tools for Learning

The statement “LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch ” shows the difference between using AI for faster learning and using it a lot so that you don’t learn anything. One of the differences between a tool and a crutch in terms of a student is that a tool allows problem solving while allowing the user to remain in control. On the other end, a crutch is help that is used without thinking twice, with the user letting the AI do all of the work. In the words of Freitas et al. (2026), “there is no way to outsource your struggle and still actually develop the skill.” On one side, excessive dependence on AI leads to cognitive debt, whereby instant answers are provided without the person actually acquiring knowledge and skills.

This compromise needs to reach an “autonomy budget” (Balzan et al., 2024). Whenever one performs any decent task like the process of essay creation, programming, or analyzing articles, there is a loss of control at play. It contributes to the transition from being able to do things yourseld to the constant dependence on the algorithm/AI. Also, AI-generated complete texts create a gap between the surface texture and the essence (Susanto et al., 2026). While an LLM enables making a paper sound polished and sophisticated, it cannot replace logical reasoning, clarity of structure, and voice of a real person.

For LLMs to remain an accessory and not a crutch, students should consider carefully when, where, and how to use LLMs:

  • WHEN to use LLMs: Use AI when you are stuck or exploring new concepts, but not when you are practicing foundational skills that you need to master yourself.

  • WHERE to use LLMs: Use AI when you are stuck or exploring new concepts, but not when you are practicing foundational skills that you need to master yourself.

  • HOW to use LLMs: Treat AI like a second set of eyes/hands rather than an answer key. Always double-check AI outputs, rewrite AI code from scratch to ensure you actually understand it, and combine basic AI prompting with core student skills like critical thinking and design.

In the End, LLMs are neither good nor bad really, everything just relies on how you use them. With proper use of AI like automation of repetitive processes, you can increase your level of knowledge without losing academic freedom.
