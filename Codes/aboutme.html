My Three Strongest Traits and Their Academic Impact

1. Persistence
This characteristic motivates me to complete challenging tasks and continue working when something is not clear to me. It increases my academic regularity since I do not give up my work just because it is difficult.
2. Graphic Design
It instructs me on visual hierarchy, organization, and awareness of the audience, which enables me to plan my essays and presentations more clearly. Making a messy design clean gives rise to my motivation of achieving order in my academic endeavors.
3. Video Editing
It develops the ability to be patient and construct narratives. Hence, my writing improves considerably. Furthermore, when I edit, it helps me to express my creativity thus relieving me of the academic burden.
